$$\large \frac{1}{B(\alpha, \beta)} x^{(\alpha-1)}(1-x)^{\beta -1}I_{(0,1)}^x$$ E a [[função beta]] é:
$\displaystyle B(x,y) = \int_0^1 t^{x-1}(1-t)^{y-1}$ 

Obs:
- É usada para modelar eventos da [[Atmosfeta]]
- usa a [[Função Gamma]]

##### Casos particulares
1) $\alpha=1,~\beta=1$    X~Beta(1+1)=U(0,1)
2) $E(x)=\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+\beta}$ e $Var(X)=\frac{\alpha\beta}{(\alpha+\beta)^2(\alpha+\beta+1)}$ 
3) Tome $y=1-x$ então X~$Beta(\beta,\alpha)$
