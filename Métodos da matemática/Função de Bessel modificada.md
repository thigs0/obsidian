$$I_v(x)=\frac{1}{i^v}J_v(ix)$$
Tomamos por definição usando as [[Funções de Bessel]].
Pois, $\displaystyle \frac{1}{i^v}J_v(ix)=\sum\limits_{k=0}^\infty \frac{(-1)^k}{k!\Gamma(k+v+1)}\left(\frac{x}{2}\right)^{2k+v}\frac{i^{2k+v}}{i^v}$ => $\displaystyle J_v(x)=\underbrace{\sum\limits_{k=0}^\infty \frac{1}{k!\Gamma(k+v+1)}\left( \frac{x}{2} \right)^{2k+v}}_{real}$
