Seja $\vec r=x\hat i+y\hat jz\hat k$

$\displaystyle \frac{\partial \vec r}{\partial q_i}=\frac{\partial \vec r}{\partial x}\frac{\partial x}{\partial q_i} + \frac{\partial \vec r}{\partial y}\frac{\partial y}{\partial q_i}+\frac{\partial \vec r}{\partial z}\frac{\partial z}{\partial q_i}$ => $\displaystyle \frac{\partial \vec r}{\partial q_i} = \frac{\partial x}{\partial q_i}e_x +  \frac{\partial y}{\partial q_i}e_y+ \frac{\partial z}{\partial q_i}e_z$ 

Normalizamos o [[Vetor]] resultado
$$||\frac{\partial \vec r}{\partial q_i}||=\sqrt{\left(\frac{\partial x}{\partial q_i}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial q_i}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial q_i}\right)^2}=h_i$$
que chamamos de [[fator de escala]] de modo que $\displaystyle\hat e_{q_i}=\frac{1}{h_i}\frac{\partial \vec r}{\partial q_i}$ 

Então dizemos que temos coordenadas curvilíneas ortogonais se e somente se $\hat e_{q_i}.\hat e_{q_i}=\delta_{ij}$ 

vejamos o [[Gradiente]] em coordenadas generalizadas

$\displaystyle \vec \nabla f = \frac{1}{h_1}\frac{\partial f}{\partial q_1}\hat e_{q_1} + \frac{1}{h_2}\frac{\partial f}{\partial q_2}\hat e_{q_2}+\frac{1}{h_3}\frac{\partial f}{\partial q_3}\hat e_{q_3}$
