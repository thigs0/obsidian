
$T=\frac{1}{2}m(\dot r^2)+r^2\dot \theta^2)$
$V=V(r)$

1) Escreva a [[Lagrangiana]]
$L=\frac{1}{2}m(\dot r^2+r^2\dot \theta^2)-V(r)$
2) Momento conjugados
$\rho_r =\frac{\partial L}{\partial \dot r}=m\dot r$, $\rho_\theta =\frac{\partial L}{\partial \dot \theta}=mr^{2}\dot \theta$ 
$\displaystyle \dot r = \frac{\rho_r}{m}$, $\displaystyle \dot \theta =\frac{\rho_\theta}{mr^2}$ 

3) Escreva L em termos de $\rho_r$ e $\rho_\theta$ 
$L=\frac{\rho_r^2}{2m}+\frac{\rho_\theta^2}{2mr^2}-V(r)$

4) Escreva $H$
$H=\rho_r \dot r + \rho_\theta \dot \theta -L$
$H=\frac{\rho_r^2}{2m}+\frac{\rho_\theta^2}{2mr^2}+V(r)$

5) **Equações Canônicas**
$\begin{align}\dot \theta =\frac{\partial H}{\partial \rho_\theta}=\frac{\rho_\theta}{mr^2}\\ \dot \rho_\theta =-\frac{\partial H}{\partial \theta}=0\end{align} \Rightarrow \rho_\theta$ é constante

$\displaystyle \begin{align}\dot r =\frac{\partial H}{\partial \rho_r}=\frac{\rho_r}{m}\\ \dot \rho_r =-\frac{\partial H}{\partial r}=-\left[-\frac{\rho_\theta^2}{mr^3}+\frac{dV}{dr}\right]=m\ddot r\end{align}\Rightarrow m\ddot r-\frac{\rho_\theta^2}{mr^2}+\frac{dV}{dr}=0$
